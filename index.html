For parents and grandparents

Big buttons for the people they love.

GrammyFone turns an iPhone into a calm, big-button phone: a large photo for everyone they call, and one red Emergency button. You set it up from your own phone, and keep it up to date from anywhere.

Download on the App Store See how it works

Free to download. GrammyFone Family is a one-time purchase, shared with your Family Sharing group.

Set it up once, from your phone.

No fiddling with settings on their phone during a visit. You pick the people and photos; GrammyFone sends it over.

On your phone

  1. Add the people they callChoose from your contacts or type them in, add a clear face photo, and sort them into Family and Friends.
  2. Send it to their phoneShow them the QR code on your screen, or print a page with the codes and the instructions already on it.
  3. Change it whenever you likeA new number or a new grandchild? Change it on your phone and theirs follows. You never have to touch their phone again.

On their phone

  1. Point the camera at the codeNo app to search for, nothing to type. The camera does the rest.
  2. Tap the big green buttonGrammyFone asks whose phone this is. One tap answers it.
  3. Tap a face to callThat's the whole phone from then on: big faces, and one red Emergency button.

No account, no password, nothing to remember. Their iPhone just needs to be signed in to iCloud.
